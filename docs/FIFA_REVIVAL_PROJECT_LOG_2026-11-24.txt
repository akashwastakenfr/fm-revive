  (hex: 0b 08 00 12 00 0c), causing ~13 repeated POST /g/game/batch retries over
  ~11 seconds until Error_RpcTimeout triggered.

5.3 Native Protobuf & Batch Handler Reversing (libFIFAMobileNeon.so.original)
- Located Haxe protobuf RPC models in .rodata:
  - com.ea.fifa.model.rpc.Batch (endpoint, payload, id, isRetry, checksum)
  - com.ea.fifa.model.rpc.BatchCollection (batchCollectionList, batchId)
  - com.ea.fifa.model.rpc.BatchResponse (status, payload, checksum)
  - com.ea.fifa.model.rpc.BatchResponseCollection (batchResponseCollectionList)
  - com.playfish.kiwi.model.system.SystemSettingsRequest (preRelease)
  - com.playfish.kiwi.model.system.SystemSettings (settingsList)
  - com.playfish.kiwi.model.system.SystemSetting (name, value)
- Disassembled BatchResponse & BatchResponseCollection (0x4b13a00..0x4b16600):
  - BatchResponseCollection: field 1 = group (0x0b..0x0c) of BatchResponse.
  - BatchResponse: field 1 (varint) = status, field 2 (bytes) = payload,
    field 3 (double on wire) = checksum.
- Disassembled SystemSetting::writeTo (0x4e55cd8..0x4e55d18) & SystemSettings
  (0x4e572dc..0x4e57658):
  - SystemSettings: field 1 = group (0x0b..0x0c) of SystemSetting.
  - SystemSetting: field 1 (string) = name, field 2 (string) = value.
- Disassembled live batch response handler (0x56f28cc..0x56f3600):
  - Confirmed at 0x56f30b8..0x56f30c8: BatchResponse.get_status() is compared
    with 0xc8 (200). Because batch-empty.bin sent status=0, every response
    branched to 0x56f3228 (RPC failure/retry path) -> Error_RpcTimeout.
  - Confirmed BatchResponse.get_checksum() (0x4b138fc) is not called in the
    0x56f3024..0x56f3360 response loop.
- Disassembled payload decoder 0x56f38b4..0x56f3af8 & 0x4e69bcc..0x4e69e0c &
  static init 0x5707720:
  - Confirmed [0x886a928] initializes to 5 (0x5707724).
  - On first BatchResponse with status=200 (while [0x886a910] is null),
    0x56f3a24..0x56f3af8 strips a 14-byte trailer (2*5 + 4 bytes) off the end
    of BatchResponse.payload, reads 4 seed bytes into 0x4e68fdc, caches
    [0x886a910], and passes the stripped ByteArray into 0x4e69bcc.
